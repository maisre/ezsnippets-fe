Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial?

Where a plan includes one, the trial length is shown on its card above.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through Paddle, our payment provider. Invoicing is available for Enterprise plans.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel from Manage Billing in your account settings — your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.
