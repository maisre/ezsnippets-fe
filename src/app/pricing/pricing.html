Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Moving up — Starter to Pro, or monthly to yearly — happens straight away and you pay only the difference for the rest of the period. Moving down to Starter takes effect at your next renewal, and you keep your current plan until then. Agency adds a team workspace when you upgrade, and can move back to Pro at the end of a billing period. During a free trial you can switch freely; the trial carries on.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Your first plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You add a card at checkout, and you won't be charged if you cancel before the trial ends. The trial is once per customer, so a later subscription starts billing straight away.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through Paddle, our payment provider.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel from Manage Billing in your account settings — your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.
