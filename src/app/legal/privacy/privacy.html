What we collect and why

The guiding principle is to collect only what we actually need to run the Service. In practice:

Identity and access

When you sign up we ask for an email address, a username, and a password. We store the password only as a bcrypt hash — we never hold the plain text, and we can't tell you what your password is. We use your email to sign you in, to reset your password, and to send you things you need to know about your account.

Billing information

If you subscribe, your payment details go straight to Paddle.com, our merchant of record. Full card numbers never reach our servers. What we store is the plan you're on, its status and renewal date, and the brand and last four digits of the card, so we can show you what you're paying for and help when billing goes wrong.

Your content

We store the pages, layouts, snippet configurations, text, and images you create or upload, because storing them is the product. Uploaded images are kept in cloud object storage and served through a content delivery network. We keep this content for as long as your account is active.

Organization membership

We record which organizations you belong to, your role in them, and who invited whom, so that access control works.

Technical and log data

We log IP addresses on sign-up and sign-in, for security and to deal with abusive registrations. We collect browser and device information and error diagnostics when something breaks, so we can fix it. Error reports can incidentally include fragments of what you were working on at the moment of the failure; we keep them briefly and then discard them.

Correspondence

When you email support, we keep the thread so we have context next time you write.

Cookies

We use cookies and browser storage that are necessary to run the Service — principally to keep you signed in and to carry your editing session between the app and the page editor. Paddle sets its own cookies during checkout. We don't run advertising cookies and we don't track you across other sites.

When we access or share your information

Service providers. A handful of companies process data on our behalf so that EZ Snippet can run at all:

  • Paddle.com — merchant of record: checkout, payments, tax, invoicing, and the billing portal.
  • Amazon Web Services — hosting, databases, and storage for uploaded images.
  • Sentry — error monitoring and diagnostics.
  • Our email provider — password resets, billing notices, and other transactional email.

Human access. Nobody here reads your content as a matter of routine. The exceptions are the same short list as in our Terms of Service: helping with a support request you've made and consented to, repairing an automated process that failed partway through, investigating abuse, and complying with a valid legal order.

Legal requests. We don't hand over data on request. We hand it over when we're compelled by a legally binding order, and we tell the affected account holder unless we're prohibited from doing so. If a tax authority audits us, we disclose the minimum billing information the audit requires and nothing more.

Aggregated data. We may aggregate or de-identify usage information and use it to understand how the Service is being used. That data can't be traced back to you.

If the company changes hands. We have no plans to sell or merge. If it ever happened, we'd tell you before your personal data moved to anyone else or came under a different privacy notice.

Your rights over your information

We apply these to everyone, wherever you live, rather than only to the people whose local law demands it:

  • To know what we collect and what we do with it.
  • To access the personal data we hold about you.
  • To correct anything inaccurate.
  • To erasure — to have your data deleted. Deleting some of it means the Service stops working for you, so this usually means closing the account.
  • To portability — to get your data out in a usable form and take it elsewhere.
  • To restrict or object to processing in certain circumstances.
  • To complain to your data protection authority. If you're in the UK or EEA, you can go to them directly, and you don't have to come to us first.
  • Not to be treated differently for exercising any of the above. We won't charge you more or give you worse support for it.

Email support@ez-snippets.com to exercise any of these and we'll respond within the time applicable law allows. We may need to verify who you are first — usually by confirming you control the account's email address.

How we secure your data

Traffic between you and us is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Access to production data is limited to the people who need it to operate the Service. Backups are encrypted.

No system is perfectly secure and we'd rather say so than imply otherwise. If a breach affects your personal data, we'll notify you and the relevant authority.

What happens when you delete things

When you delete a page, layout, or uploaded asset, it stops being accessible in the app straight away. It may remain on active servers for up to 30 days and in backups for up to 60 days after that. Within 90 days it's gone from everything.

If you close your account, your content becomes inaccessible immediately and is purged from our systems within 60 days. We can't recover any of it after that, so export anything you want to keep first.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it. Account and content data lasts as long as the account. Error diagnostics are kept for a short period and then discarded. Billing and tax records we have to keep longer, because the law requires it — typically seven years.

Where your data lives

EZ Snippet runs on infrastructure in the United States. If you're in the UK, the EEA, or anywhere else outside the US, your information will be transferred to and stored in the United States. Where those transfers need a legal basis, they're covered by Standard Contractual Clauses with the providers involved.

Changes and questions

We'll update this notice as the Service changes and as regulations require. When a change is significant, we'll update the date at the top and tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.

Questions, concerns, or a request about your data? Email support@ez-snippets.com — a real person reads it.
