Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated August 24, 2026

We'd rather deal with a refund request honestly than hide behind fine print. The principle we work to is simple: if you paid for a period of EZ Snippet and didn't get value out of it, we'll make it right. If you did get value out of it, we won't refund it — and we'll tell you so directly rather than going quiet.

This policy covers subscriptions to EZ Snippet sold by EzSnippets LLC. Payments are processed by Paddle.com, our merchant of record, and any refund goes back through Paddle to the card or account you paid with.

Cancelling is separate from refunding

You can cancel whenever you like, from Manage Billing in your account settings. No form to fill in, no reason required, no phone call. Cancelling stops the next renewal — it doesn't cut short the period you've already paid for. Your plan keeps working, in full, until that period ends.

We don't automatically prorate the period you're in the middle of when you cancel. If you think your situation warrants a partial refund, ask us — the examples below are the sort of thing we'd say yes to.

If your plan came with a free trial, cancelling before the trial ends means you're never charged at all.

Refunds we'd grant in full

  • You meant to cancel, the renewal charge went through anyway, and you haven't used the account since. Tell us and we'll refund that charge.
  • You forgot about the subscription entirely — it renewed a few times, and the account has been dormant through all of it. We'll refund the dormant periods.
  • You subscribed, tried to build something, and the product genuinely couldn't do what you needed. Contact us within your first billing period and we'll refund it.
  • You were charged twice for the same period, or charged on a plan you'd already cancelled. That's our mistake and we'll fix it.

Refunds and credits we'd consider in part

  • The subscription renewed, you used it lightly for a week or two, then stopped. We'd look at the account and refund the portion you didn't use.
  • You upgraded to a higher plan and never touched the capacity you upgraded for. We'd usually credit the difference toward future months.
  • We had meaningful downtime — several hours in a day, or repeated outages across a month — or you emailed support and we left you hanging for days. We'd credit the account for that.

Refunds we wouldn't grant

This is the part most policies leave vague, so we'll be plain about it. We generally won't refund a period where the account clearly got what it paid for:

  • You built, edited, or published pages and layouts during the period, or exported a site. That's the product working as intended, and changing your mind afterwards isn't grounds for a refund.
  • You used the period heavily and requested a refund at the end of it.
  • You subscribed to get past a plan limit, got past it, and then asked for the money back.
  • You downgraded mid-period. The lower rate starts at your next renewal; we don't refund the difference for the period already paid for.
  • Charges from someone else — stock imagery licensed directly from a provider, your own domain registration, your own hosting. We never received that money and can't return it.
  • Your account was terminated for a material breach of our Terms of Service.

How we work out whether you got value

When you ask for a refund, we look at what actually happened on the account during the period in question — whether pages or layouts were created or edited, whether anything was published or exported, whether assets were uploaded. We're looking at activity, not reading your content.

Put plainly: a quiet account gets refunded, a busy one doesn't, and the middle gets prorated. Where it's genuinely borderline, we'll lean your way once.

Your legal rights come first

Nothing here limits refund or cancellation rights you have under consumer law where you live, including any statutory cooling-off or withdrawal period. Where the law gives you more than this policy does, the law wins.

How to ask

Email support@ez-snippets.com from the address on your account and tell us what happened and roughly when. You don't need to build a case — a couple of sentences is plenty. We aim to reply within two business days.

Approved refunds are issued by Paddle and usually land within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. You can also request a refund directly from the Paddle receipt emailed to you after each payment.

Anything that doesn't fit the examples above, we handle case by case. Get in touch and we'll work it out with you.
