Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026

30-day money-back guarantee. Ask us within 30 days of any charge and we'll refund it in full. No reason required, no conditions, and we won't ask what you did with the account.

This policy covers subscriptions to EZ Snippet sold by EzSnippets LLC. Payments are processed by Paddle.com, our merchant of record, and any refund goes back through Paddle to the card or account you paid with.

The 30 days

The window is 30 calendar days from the date of the charge, and it applies to each charge on its own — your first payment, and every renewal after it. A renewal you'd forgotten about is refundable for 30 days from the day it was taken, not from the day you first subscribed.

Cancelling is separate from refunding

You can cancel whenever you like, from Manage Billing in your account settings. No form to fill in, no reason required, no phone call. Cancelling stops the next renewal; on its own it doesn't hand back the period you've already paid for, so if you want that period refunded too, just ask us within the 30 days.

If your plan came with a free trial, cancelling before the trial ends means you're never charged at all.

How to ask

Email support@ez-snippets.com from the address on your account. One sentence is plenty — you don't need to explain yourself or build a case. We aim to reply within two business days.

You can also request a refund directly from the Paddle receipt emailed to you after each payment.

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, within 14 days of approval where that method allows it. Your bank may take a few days longer to show it.

Your legal rights come first

Nothing here limits refund or cancellation rights you have under consumer law where you live, including any statutory cooling-off or withdrawal period. Where the law gives you more than this policy does, the law wins.
